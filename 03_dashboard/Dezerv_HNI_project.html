Why the goal changes the recommendation

Same assets. Different job.For wealth compounding, the current growth orientation is closer to the required risk budget. The main issue is duplicated exposure, not lack of growth.
For retirement, 71.1% growth is materially more aggressive.The illustrative target falls to 55%, shifting capital toward debt and liquidity.
For the ₹35L outlay, the liability gets its own bucket.The workbook calculates an indicative present value of ₹27.21L at 6.5% over four years.
